Ponto de Luz Residence

Use: Residential

Location: Rogério Pereira de Camargo St.

Area: 110 m²

Located between the peaceful, green streets of Adari Fernando Vizinoni and Rogério Pereira de Camargo, this project stands out with its contemporary style, featuring asymmetries on its facade and organic shapes in its garden to break the balance of the abundant surrounding nature. The architecture dialogues harmoniously with the environment, creating a space that not only shelters but also celebrates the natural beauty nearby. The use of sustainable, high-quality materials reflects a commitment to the environment, ensuring the residence integrates sustainably into the landscape.

Designed for a childless couple, the house focuses on spaciousness: multiple windows provide stunning views of nature and allow significant sunlight entry, especially in areas where residents spend most of their time. This open-space concept promotes a welcoming atmosphere and invites nature indoors.

Furthermore, the integration of the living room + kitchen + dining area reinforces the sense of extension, promoting interaction and conviviality in a space that feels both intimate and expansive. Minimalist design elements and functional furniture complement the home's contemporary aesthetic.

Use: Residential

Location: Dr. Aluízio França St.

Area: 500 m²

Meandros Residence, strategically located in front of the iconic Barigui Park, draws inspiration from the beautiful curves of the park's central lake.

Spanning an impressive 500 m², Meandros offers comfort, harmony, and natural beauty, perfectly blending sophistication with nature. Its curved design, generous spaces, and attention to detail make it an exceptional choice for a family of four seeking elegant living within a stunning natural setting.

With a privileged location and high-standard amenities, this residence serves as a natural refuge amidst urban chaos. Its organic forms successfully integrate the exterior nature with the internal environment, creating an immersion of the building within the surrounding greenery.
